ABOUT THIS APPLICATION

Campus Library

A library operations and continuous-improvement workspace.

What it supports

Catalog and copy management, member accounts, circulation, reservations, fines, feedback, complaints, quality analysis, improvement actions, PDCA cycles, inventory audits, and operational reports.

Dashboard indicators are calculated from records in this installation. A missing value means there is not enough data to calculate that measure; displayed sample designs should not be treated as live metrics.

Your account role controls which pages and records you can access.
